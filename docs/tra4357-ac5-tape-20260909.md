## Mechanism: the OTM spot path is a pure cache read behind a breaker that is open ~96% of the time

I paired `/api/health/quotes` with `/api/health/rv-scan` in the same poll to time-align source state against cycle outcome. This **falsified** the obvious explanation and produced the real one.

The obvious hypothesis -- "both quote sources dark" -- is the one the TRA-4357 fix comment itself predicts (`index.ts:1268-1273`). **It does not hold.** Across 27 time-aligned cycles:

- Yahoo breaker **open on 27 of 27**
- Tradier quote-path breaker **open on 26 of 27**, `reason: "quota"`, `quotePathOpen: true`
- ...on the 100% cycles **and on the healthy cycles alike**

Breaker state does not discriminate. Five consecutive cycles at 16:07:58-16:08:18 resolved spot for ~99.6% of their universe *with both breakers open*. Nor does cycle spacing: the healthy run sits mid-burst with 2-8s gaps, same as the 100% cycles around it.

What does discriminate is **quote-cache freshness**, and the code makes the all-or-nothing shape inevitable:

1. `fetchSpot` (`index.ts:1250-1283`) is `fetchQuotes([symbol], { maxStaleMs: CHAIN_CACHE_TTL_MS })`. Post-TRA-4357 that is a 60s bound. It has **no fallback**: cache miss returns `null`, and `scanOtm` maps `null` straight to `no_spot` (`relative-value-scanner.ts:611`).
2. `quoteCache` (`yahoo-feed.ts:700`) is TTL-only and is **written in batches** -- `for (const [sym, q] of entries) quoteCache.set(sym, { quote: q, storedAt: now })` (`yahoo-feed.ts:737`), one `now` for the whole batch.
3. So every entry from a given refill **expires at the same instant**. The population is either almost-all-fresh or almost-all-stale. That is the bimodality, exactly.
4. With the quote path breaker open on 26 of 27 observations, refills are rare and brief. Between them nothing can repopulate the cache, so the OTM sleeve goes to 100% `no_spot` and stays there until the next successful batch.

**TRA-4357 widened the bound from 15s to 60s. That tripled the post-refill hit window, which is why the healthy baseline improved from 100% to ~0.5% and why AC1/AC2 should not be reverted.** It did not touch, and could not touch, the reason refills are rare: the quote quota is exhausted. Universes in this sample reached **751 names** -- well past the 364->568 growth that was TRA-1996's root cause.

So: AC1/AC2 fixed a real defect. AC5 fails anyway, because it grades an outcome that depends on an upstream budget nobody has fixed yet.
