### The premise behind the 100% needs correcting first

`costR` is a **TRA-3483 RECORDER**. The deployed flat form compares

```
admit  ⟺  lowerCI95(cell) ≥ barR      i.e.  grossR ≥ barR
```

and never reads `costR` on that branch. Two consequences:

- `grossR` is a property of the **cell**, not the candidate, so within a cell on a day the predicate is **constant** — block rate is 0% or 100%, never between. The 100.0% rows are the expected shape.
- `byReason`'s `shortfall_*` is **`barR − grossR`**, not a shortfall of cost against anything.

### The actual cause, and it is not the edge estimator

`arm.admissibleStrike.band` reads **`{ min: 0.25, max: 0.40 }`** (`OTM_ADMISSIBLE_DELTA_MIN=0.25`, `OTM_ADMISSIBLE_DELTA_MAX=0.40`).

The TRA-3401 nominator selects into `|Δ| ∈ [0.25, 0.40)` — **exactly** the two cells measuring **−0.0631** and **−0.2154** — and structurally excludes `0.50-0.55`, the only cell on the tape measuring positive (mean **+1.1291**, lo95 **0.3617**, n=110) and the only band the TRA-3392 sleeve mandate **authorizes** (`[0.495, 0.55)`).

The nominator band and the mandate's authorized band are **disjoint** (0.40 vs 0.495). `bySelection` corroborates: **8763 of 8925** rows on `in_band_fair` — the selector clamped essentially the whole population. `0.50-0.55` was the entire evaluated population on 08-26..28 (4159 rows, **60.9% admitted**) and has not been nominated since 08-29.

**`cost_bar` is working as designed.** It is refusing a population the nominator should not have offered it. The 13-session dark run is a selector/mandate alignment problem, not a gate-tuning one.

### This prices TRA-1661 at zero

`barR = max(costModel + safetyMargin, minGrossR)` and **`minGrossR = 0.3`** — the bar cannot fall below 0.3 by any cost-side retune. The nominated cells sit at −0.0631 and −0.2154. No cost retune, no `k`, no margin cut opens a single currently-nominated cell. The only cell a retune reaches is `0.50-0.55` at 0.3617, which already clears the 0.3 floor and is excluded by the band, not the bar.
